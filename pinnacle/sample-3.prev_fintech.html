Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Pinnacle's 3-tier journey and platform rules.

Do I need to refer or recruit anyone to receive payouts?
No. Pinnacle has 0 referral requirements anywhere across the tiers. Positions advance based strictly on global FIFO (First-In, First-Out) time ordering as new participants join or existing members automatically progress.
How does progression work between the 3 tiers?
Level 1 entry is $300 and delivers $1,200 payout. To move to Level 2, members pay a $300 slot reservation. In Level 2, members receive $6,000 payout, and pay $1,500 to advance to the Level 3 Peak Tier ($3,000).
What happens when someone completes Level 3?
Level 3 is the terminal Peak Tier. Members who reach this final tier cannot upgrade anymore—all liquidity settles directly into net member cash.
Is real money required to test the platform?
No. Pinnacle operates in a full simulation Sandbox environment. You can explore all registrations, tier transitions, and payout calculators with zero financial risk.
