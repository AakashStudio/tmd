Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Pinnacle's 7-level journey and platform rules.

Do I need to refer or recruit anyone to receive payouts?
No. Pinnacle has 0 referral requirements anywhere across all 7 levels. Positions advance based strictly on global FIFO (First-In, First-Out) time ordering as new participants join or existing members automatically progress.
How does automated progression between levels work?
When your group of 6 contributions completes at your current level, the gross payout funds three distinct streams: (1) your cash payout, (2) the community support cushion pool, and (3) your automated entry reservation into the next tier.
What happens when someone completes Level 7?
Because there is no Level 8, Level 7 features a cyclical recirculation mechanism. A portion of the payout re-enters Level 1 at the base of the community, driving liquidity back to waiting members.
Is real money required to test the platform?
No. Pinnacle operates in a full simulation Sandbox environment. You can explore all registrations, level transitions, and payout calculators with zero financial risk.
